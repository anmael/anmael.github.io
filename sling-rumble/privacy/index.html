Sling Rumble Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · Operated by x4nm43l

This policy explains what Sling Rumble handles when you play on Android, what other players can see, and how to ask us to delete your account.

Practice and device settings

You can play Practice without making a Sling Rumble account. The app saves practice progress, loadouts, and preferences on your device. Android may include some app data in device backup or transfers if you enable those features. To remove the local copy, use Android’s app storage settings or uninstall the app. That does not delete an online account.

Google sign-in and online play

If you sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication are used to authenticate you. The app can access your Google account email and uses a Firebase user ID to associate you with your Sling Rumble profile. We store your player name, account and matchmaking identifiers, rank and match results, match actions, loadouts, gear, cosmetics and entitlements, and in-game balances. If you use Player Codes or gifting, we use the code and related gift records to find the right account and verify the gift.

Other signed-in players can see player names and standings on the leaderboard. The leaderboard data also contains app-specific player IDs. Opponents can see your player name, appearance, and match activity. Your Google account email is not shown on the leaderboard or to opponents. Please do not use a real name, email address, or other personal information as your player name.

We use this information to sign you in, save progress, run matches, display standings, deliver items, investigate problems, and protect the service from abuse. Google services, including Firebase, process data to provide authentication and online features. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Optional rewarded ads

When available, signed-in players age 18 or older can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to earn in-game Style Points. Ads are not required to play. The app stores the result of the ad age screen on your device and does not send your age selection as part of the reward request. Google’s consent tool shows privacy choices when required. Where available, you can reopen those choices in the ad reward area.

When you use this feature, Google AdMob and its consent tool process information for ad delivery, measuring ad activity, and preventing fraud. This can include your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance or diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the app set ID. To confirm a reward, the game sends Google your Sling Rumble account identifier (your Firebase user ID) and a one-time reward verification code. Google returns transaction details used to verify the reward and prevent duplicate credits. We do not send your Google account email as part of that reward verification. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Play purchases

When available, eligible signed-in players can buy cosmetic items through Google Play. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Sling Rumble does not receive your full card number or other payment credentials. The app sends Google Play a coded version of your Sling Rumble account identifier so the purchase is linked to the right game account.

We process the item and product identifiers, purchase token, purchase status, and related order information to verify purchases with Google Play, unlock or restore items on the correct Sling Rumble account, prevent duplicate grants, and handle cancellations or refunds. We store purchase and entitlement records, including hashed references to tokens and order IDs, for these purposes. To restore an eligible purchase, use the original Google Play account and the Sling Rumble account that received it. Refunded or canceled purchases can lose their associated unlock. You can request a refund through Google Play. Deleting your Sling Rumble account does not cancel or refund a purchase and does not delete your Google Play order history.

Children’s privacy

Practice does not require sign-in. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided personal information to Sling Rumble, contact us using the link below to ask to review or delete it, or to stop further collection. Please do not send a child’s birth date, password, or identity documents in your first message.

Retention and deletion

We keep your online profile and associated gameplay data while they are needed to run your account and provide online play. For instructions and details about what may be kept after a request, see Delete your Sling Rumble account.

Support website

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the deletion information page. We do not ask you to enter an account identifier on these pages or add tracking scripts. GitHub may process connection information to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
