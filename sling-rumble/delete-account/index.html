Delete your Sling Rumble account

Deleting your account is permanent. You won’t be able to recover your online progress, presets, balances, items, cosmetics, or rank. It affects your Sling Rumble account across installations that use that account. It does not delete your Google account.

Request deletion without the app

Email an account deletion request

If possible, send the request from the email address you used to sign in. You can include your player name if you know it. We will check that you are authorized to delete the account before acting. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us for help. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, receipts, payment card details, or identity documents.

Request deletion in the app

In a version that offers in-app deletion, open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. Sign in again to confirm ownership, then enter DELETE. If you do not see this option, use the email request above.

What is removed

We remove your Sling Rumble sign-in record, profile, leaderboard entry, queue and player-code information, and account-linked game progress and items. We remove information that identifies you from shared match records, so your former opponents can keep their match history. We remove your bot-only match history.

What may remain

We may keep limited records needed to complete the deletion safely, prevent duplicate rewards or purchases, or meet legal or security obligations. Duplicate-prevention records do not include your player name or email. Google may retain its own account or order records separately; deleting Sling Rumble does not delete your Google account or Google Play order history. If we need to keep identifiable information for a specific obligation, we limit it to what that obligation requires.

Local Practice saves or settings may remain on your device. You can remove them in Android’s app storage settings. For more about how we handle data, read the Sling Rumble Privacy Policy.
